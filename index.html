Your first visit

Nothing to prepare. Bring flat-soled training shoes and a towel.

Step 1

Intro session

45 minutes with a coach. A movement screen, a talk about your goals, and a first plan you can use that day.

Step 2

Cool down on us

Finish in the steam room or lounge, then a staff member walks you through the sauna, cold plunge and massage rooms.

Step 3

Pick a plan

Start on Unwind, Restore or Recharge. You can move up or down a level at the start of any month.

Book your free first session

Tell us what you want to work on and we will email you times that suit you. No payment details are needed.

When do I use my massages?

Book through your account up to 14 days ahead. Most members book the slot right after training. Sessions are 30 or 45 minutes, with a sports or relaxation focus, and unused massages do not carry over to the next month.

Do I need experience?

No. The intro session starts from where you are, and most coaches here work with first-time lifters every week.

Can I cancel or switch plans?

Monthly plans cancel with 30 days' notice. You can move between levels at the start of any month, and any plan can be frozen for up to two months a year.

What should I bring?

Flat-soled training shoes and a towel, plus swimwear for the sauna and cold plunge. Robes and slippers are provided.

You can choose a plan now or after your intro. Nothing is charged today.
